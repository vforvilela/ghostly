An entry session of a group's link whose WebRTC attempt connected nothing goes on over a native transport, as an edge does: it runs its native endpoints, its `_tr` lists no `webrtc/1`, and the other side follows. Before, entry sessions stayed WebRTC only: a joiner or an admin on a network that lets no direct connection through (a VPN, a firewall) saw the other side's packet and never got the welcome, and the join stayed at "Let in: getting the group's keys".
